About the group

The Research Cluster of Communication Networks and Systems investigates how information moves across space — reliably, securely, and efficiently. Our work spans the full stack: from spectrum policy and dynamic sharing, through modulation, coding and estimation theory, up to machine-learning-driven radios and 6G network design.

Our members graduate fluent in both the mathematics of communication and the systems that make it physical.

We collaborate with academic and industry partners across telecom and beyond, and welcome motivated students at every level.
